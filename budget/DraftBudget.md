# Draft Budget

## Nairobi AI Safety Hackathon

**Expected attendance:** 50 participants
**Duration:** 2 days
**Location:** Nairobi

| Item                            | Estimated cost (KES) |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------: |
| Venue                           |               90,000 |
| Catering                        |              120,000 |
| Internet                        |               20,000 |
| Participant materials           |               25,000 |
| Prizes                          |              100,000 |
| Participant travel support      |               50,000 |
| Mentor & judges facilitation    |               60,000 |
| Printing and miscellaneous      |               20,000 |
| Contingency                     |               15,000 |
| **Total**                       |          **500,000** |

## Budget notes

### Venue

Estimated cost for a two-day venue with tables, chairs, electricity, and reliable internet access.

### Catering

Breakfast, lunch, and refreshments for approximately 50 participants over two days.

### Internet

Additional connectivity to ensure participants can access model APIs, GitHub, documentation, and other tools during the hackathon.

### Participant materials

Basic supplies and materials required during the event.

### Prizes

Small prizes for selected projects. The purpose is to recognize strong work rather than make the event primarily competitive.

### Participant travel support

Limited support for participants travelling to Nairobi who would otherwise have difficulty attending.

### Mentors and Judges accommodation

Support for mentors travelling from outside Nairobi where necessary.

### Documentation

Basic event photography and documentation of projects.

### Miscellaneous

Printing, signage, adapters, cables, and other small event costs.

### Contingency

A small reserve for unexpected expenses and price changes.

## Cost reduction

We will look for in-kind support where possible.

Potential contributions include:

* Venue
* Internet
* Software credits
* Model API credits
* Food
* Equipment
* Mentorship

Any confirmed in-kind support will be declared and would reduce the amount of funding required.
